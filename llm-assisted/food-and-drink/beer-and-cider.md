## Beverage Preferences: Beer and Cider

### Beer
Daniel is a connoisseur of dark beers, particularly Guinness, and other non-carbonated stouts. He is also an IPA enthusiast, favoring the most bitter options available, often choosing those with the highest IBU (International Bittering Units). Daniel prefers beers with a slightly higher alcohol content, typically in the 6-8% ABV range, but occasionally indulges in stronger brews up to 8% ABV. He exclusively orders pints when at bars and will opt for a liter if available, even if the beer itself is not of the highest quality.

### Cider
Daniel's cider preferences lean towards very dry, almost completely unsweetened varieties, which he acknowledges can be hard to find. He also enjoys pear ciders.
