## Podcast Preferences: Technology Focus

### Themes
Daniel is interested in podcasts covering technology, particularly those discussing large language models and generative AI. He prefers content that takes a long-term, conservative, and skeptical view of these technologies, focusing on mature evaluations of practical use cases.

### Specific Interests
- Enterprise and scaled-up applications of generative AI.
- Use cases emphasizing data access and intelligibility, especially in the field of sustainability finance.
- Real-world problem-solving with AI, steering away from the more glamorous, consumer-focused use cases like text-to-video or text-to-image generation.
